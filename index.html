About the inn

Small on purpose

Wrenmoor Inn sits on a low bluff at the northern edge of Sallow Point, where the coast road turns inland and the pines take over. The house was built in 1926 as a family summer home and opened as an inn in 1982. It has never grown past fourteen rooms, and we would like to keep it that way.

Days here are simple. Breakfast is served on the terrace until eleven. The pool is warm from May through October. The cove below the garden is a ten-minute walk down the switchback path, and the village, with its bakery, bookshop and Saturday market, is a fifteen-minute stroll along the headland. In the evening our small restaurant serves a short menu built around what the boats and the farms brought in that morning.
